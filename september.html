The account posted every single day — and then one Reel out of eleven posts carried the month. The endoscopy film on 7 September was seen 2,343 times. Everything else put together came to 1,646.

Cadence was the easy part and it held: a post a day from the 1st to the 10th, two on the 4th, stories running alongside from the 2nd. What the month shows is that reach is not a reward for showing up. Nine of the eleven posts landed between 45 and 321 views — roughly the size of the follower list — while the one that named a symptom and a procedure was pushed to strangers by Instagram itself.

Format is not the lever. Of the four Reels, three performed like the photos did. The difference was the brief: a named symptom, a named procedure, and a reason not to wait.
